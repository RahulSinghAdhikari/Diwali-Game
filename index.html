HOW TO PLAY

Darkness has swallowed Diwalipur. Clear five quick challenges — about 90 seconds in total — and bring the festival back to life.

  1. 1

    LIGHT RUSH

    Tap every unlit diya before it fades. Golden diyas are worth more. Avoid the dark wisps.

    Diya +10 · Golden +25 · Wisp −15

  2. 2

    RANGOLI RUSH

    Look at the pattern on top, then pick the tile that matches it exactly. Four rounds, the last one is a speed bonus.

    Match +100 · Streak bonus

  3. 3

    FIREWORK FRENZY

    Tap rockets as they climb the sky. Golden ones pay double. Never tap the dark duds.

    Rocket +20 · Golden +50 · Dud −20

  4. 4

    MITHAI MANIA

    Drag (or use ← →) to move the basket and catch falling sweets. Dodge the empty boxes.

    Laddoo +10 · Jalebi +15 · Barfi +20 · Golden +50

  5. 5

    DEFEAT THE DARKNESS

    Tap the glowing weak points, drag your lamp away from falling dark energy, and fire the power-ups you collected in stages 1–4.

    Weak point · Golden core · Final strike

COMBO MULTIPLIER

Chain successful hits without a mistake to multiply every point you score.

3 hits ×1.55 hits ×210 hits ×3
